How to use it

From the first download to a working job-search workspace.

Start with one file. Once uploaded, the AI supplies every link and guides setup until the workspace is ready.

What you need
  • A laptop or desktop computer
  • A personal AI account
  • A current sanitized CV

No coding, terminal, or GitHub account required.

  1. Download the starter file

    Get MULAI_DI_SINI.md. This is the only file you need to find yourself.

  2. Upload it to your AI chat

    Upload it to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, then say: Help me get started.

  3. Choose tracker storage

    Choose local for the no-Google dashboard, or Google Sheets for access across devices.

  4. Follow the setup guide

    The AI supplies official links and complete steps for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, or a suitable local agent.

  5. Open the Personal Workspace

    Download the ZIP from the AI's exact-version link, extract it, and open the folder in the agent. No repository clone is needed.

  6. Add your CV and begin

    The agent verifies the workspace, builds an approved career profile, and becomes ready to find jobs, prepare CVs, and maintain the tracker.

After setup: ask it to find suitable jobs, paste a posting link, or request a CV for a Job ID. You still decide and submit applications yourself.

Feedback tanpa akun

Jangan masukkan CV, email recruiter, detail lamaran, alamat, nomor telepon, atau informasi pribadi lain. Email kontak hanya diperlukan kalau lo ingin mendapat balasan.

Account-free feedback

Your experience can be honest and anonymous.

Do not include a CV, recruiter email, application details, address, phone number, or other personal information. A contact email is needed only if you want a reply.
